# ORP Hub — User guide

ORP Hub works on any phone or computer browser. Sign in with your email and password. On a phone, use the bar at the bottom to move between sections; on a computer, use the menu on the left. The 🔔 bell shows your notifications.

Your **Dashboard** always shows an **Inbox** of what needs your attention, most urgent first. If it says "You're all caught up", there is nothing waiting for you.

---

## For the Resident Pastor

### Approving money
1. Requests waiting for you appear on the Dashboard as **Approve: …** and on **Finance → Requests**.
2. Open a request to see the amount, fund, purpose and history.
3. Choose one:
   - **Approve** — commits the money from the fund. The finance officer is notified to pay.
   - **Ask for clarification** — type your question; the request goes back to the person who asked. When they reply it returns to your inbox.
   - **Reject** — give a reason; the requester is told.
4. If the fund doesn't have enough money available, the Approve button is disabled and you'll see why. Ask the finance officer to add budget, or ask for clarification to reduce the amount.

Approved requests can't be changed afterwards. Only the finance officer can record payments.

### Your diary
- **Pastor → Overview** shows today, the next 7 days, approvals waiting, follow-ups due, and work you've delegated.
- **Meetings → Pastor's diary** lists every booking with the person's purpose. You can reschedule, cancel, and after a meeting mark it **held** or **no-show**.
- **Meetings → Meeting notes**: record notes and decisions. Tick **Confidential** for notes only you can see. Use **Create follow-up task** to assign action points.

### Pastoral care
- **Pastor → Pastoral follow-ups**: people to visit or call, with a "remind me by" date. Due items appear on your dashboard. **Delegate** turns one into a task for someone else.
- **Pastor → Prayer & testimonies**: requests from the church team. Mark **Praying** or **Answered**; share testimonies with the team. Confidential requests are visible only to you.
- **Pastor → Private notes**: visible only to you — not the assistant, not the administrator.

### Reports
Open **Reports → All ministry reports** to read submitted reports. **Mark reviewed** or **Request revision**. Any answer can become a task with **→ make task**. **Consolidated summary** totals every ministry's figures for a period; you can print it or save it as PDF.

## For the Pastor's Assistant

### Managing availability
1. **Meetings → Availability**.
2. Choose a starting date, the time window (e.g. 10:00–13:00), the slot length, and how many weeks to repeat it.
3. Click **Publish slots**. ORP Hub skips any times that overlap existing slots.
4. To take a time off the calendar, **Block** it (or **Remove** it). Booked slots can't be removed — cancel the appointment first.

### Booking for someone
Go to **Book an appointment**, pick a slot, and choose **Booking for: a staff member** or **a visitor (no account)**. Staff members get a confirmation notification.

### Reports
1. Reports → **Templates**: install the standard templates once (Monthly Ministry Report, Event Report, Quarterly Review). You can edit questions or create your own.
2. Click **Request reports**: choose the template, the period (e.g. "October 2026"), the due date and the ministries. Each leader is notified.
3. **All ministry reports** shows what's submitted, pending and overdue. Review or request revisions.

### Follow-ups and prayer requests
Same as the pastor, except confidential items are hidden from you.

## For the Finance Officer

### Setting up funds
1. **Finance → New fund** for each budget line (e.g. "Welfare 2026"), with an optional opening budget.
2. **Allocate budget** increases or reduces a fund's budget. You can't reduce it below what's already approved.
3. **Record income** adds money received into a fund (e.g. a special offering) — include the teller or receipt number.

### Paying approved requests
Approved requests appear on your Dashboard as **Pay: …**.
1. Open the request and click **Record payment**. Enter the amount (part-payments are fine), the date, method and the **receipt / transfer reference**. You can paste a link to a scanned receipt (e.g. Google Drive).
2. When the full amount is paid, the request becomes **Paid — completed**.
3. **Pay advance** is for money given before receipts. When the person accounts for it, use **Record refund** for any unspent cash, then **Close request**.
4. **Close request** releases any unpaid balance back to the fund (e.g. the job cost less than approved).

ORP Hub will not let you pay more than was approved.

### Ledger, reconciliation and reports
- **Ledger** is the permanent record of every money movement. Entries can't be edited or deleted; correct mistakes with a refund or a budget adjustment.
- **Reconciliations**: monthly, enter your bank (or petty-cash) statement balance. ORP Hub shows the expected balance from its records and the difference; explain any difference in the note.
- **Reports**: choose a date range for totals by fund and category. **Download CSV** opens in Excel; **Print / save as PDF** gives a printable summary.

## For ministry leaders and staff

- **Ask for money:** Finance → **New request**. Choose the fund, amount, and explain the purpose with a breakdown. You'll be notified when it's approved, rejected, or if the pastor needs more information (then use **Respond and resubmit**). You can **Withdraw** a request before it's decided.
- **Book the pastor:** Meetings → **Book an appointment** → pick a time → say what it's about. Use **📅 Add to calendar** so your phone reminds you. You can reschedule or cancel from **My appointments**.
- **Reports:** when a report is requested you'll see it on your Dashboard. Fill it in, **Save draft** if you need to come back, then **Submit report**. If a revision is requested, update and submit again.
- **Tasks:** Tasks assigned to you are on your Dashboard and under **Tasks**. Change the status to In progress or Done.
- **Prayer requests and testimonies:** Pastor → **Share a prayer request or testimony**. Tick **Confidential** if only the Resident Pastor should see it.

## For the administrator

- New people sign up themselves; they wait on a "Waiting for activation" screen. Open **Admin → People & roles**, click the person, tick their role(s) and **Activate**.
- To remove access, untick **Account active**.
- **Admin → Ministries**: set each ministry's leader (they receive report requests).
- **Admin → Audit history**: who did what, when. It can't be edited.
- **Admin → Backup**: download a backup at least monthly and keep it somewhere safe. See [Backup & recovery](BACKUP_AND_RECOVERY.md).

Forgot password? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
